We are Closed as of October 2, 2026

It is with a heavy heart that we announce the closure of Cumoratek AV Solutions on October 2, 2026. For almost a decade, our family-owned & operated company has taken pride in serving the Kansas City community and providing affordable Audio/Visual and IT services for organizations of all sizes. Our core value of client advocacy has been at the center of our mission from Day 1 and has led us to work with clients all around the Midwest, including Missouri, Kansas, Nebraska, Iowa, and Illinois.

We have made the difficult decision to cease operations effective October 2, 2026. This decision does not come lightly, and we will deeply miss the relationships we have developed with our clients over the past decade.

We are deeply appreciative of each of the organizations who have trusted Cumoratek over the years and we wish you all the best on your journey!
